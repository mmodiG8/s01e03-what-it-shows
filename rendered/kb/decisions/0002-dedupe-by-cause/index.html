0002: Merge reports only after verifying the cause

owner mmodiG8 accepted reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Date: 29 October 2026. Decider: mmodiG8. Supersedes: decision 0001.

Context

The review of #4471 found different causes hidden inside one known ticket. Matching subjects neither establish the same failure nor exclude a different one. The former accepted record remains unchanged as history, not as the current merge policy.

Decision

Read each report and verify its cause before merging. For the date failures, look up the tenant locale and booking-form version and inspect the channel using causeOf and sameCause in src/lib/kb/causes.ts. Browser intake tags and normalized subjects are hints, not authority. Matching subjects may point to different tickets, and different subjects may describe the same cause. Unknown tenant or insufficient evidence means no automatic merge; investigate first.

Consequences

Triage takes more reading but preserves separate engineering fixes and honest counts. Deduplicate exports by report ID, not by subject or raw row count. Re-evaluate open known tickets as reports arrive. Treat classifier results as the exported model, and retain contrary body evidence for investigation rather than claiming a cause the data does not establish.
